## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Ice To Meet You**.

We value your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is collected, used, and protected when you play our game.

This Privacy Policy describes the data practices associated with Ice To Meet You, including our use of Google Firebase Analytics to understand gameplay performance and improve the player experience.

Ice To Meet You is currently available without advertisements or in-app purchases.

## 2. Information We Collect

Ice To Meet You uses **Google Firebase Analytics**, a service provided by Google LLC, to collect information about gameplay activity and application performance.

### 2.1 Gameplay Analytics

Depending on the events implemented in the game, we may collect information such as:

- Game launches and sessions.
- Session duration and frequency.
- Levels started, completed, or failed.
- Battle outcomes and gameplay progression.
- Hero unlocks and upgrades.
- In-game item usage.
- In-game currency activity.
- Tutorial progression and completion.
- Gameplay interactions and feature usage.

These events help us understand how players interact with Ice To Meet You and identify opportunities to improve the experience.

### 2.2 Device and Technical Information

Firebase Analytics automatically collects certain technical information, which may include:

- Device model and operating system.
- Operating system version.
- Application version.
- Approximate geographic location derived from technical information.
- App-instance identifiers.
- Advertising identifiers where available and permitted by device settings and applicable regulations.
- Application usage statistics.

The information collected depends on Firebase configuration, device capabilities, and applicable privacy settings.

### 2.3 Personal Information

Ice To Meet You does not require players to provide their real names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses for standard gameplay.

Although we do not intentionally request this information through gameplay analytics, technical identifiers and other analytics information may qualify as personal data under applicable privacy laws.

## 3. How We Use Collected Information

We use analytics information to:

- Monitor gameplay engagement.
- Measure tutorial and level completion rates.
- Improve game balance and difficulty.
- Identify unpopular or confusing gameplay features.
- Analyze player progression and retention.
- Monitor application usage patterns.
- Improve overall gameplay quality and performance.

We do not use Firebase Analytics to display advertisements in the current version of Ice To Meet You.

## 7. Data Storage and Security

Firebase Analytics information is transmitted to and processed through Google's analytics infrastructure.

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control, including limiting access to analytics reports to authorized personnel.

Google maintains security measures for its analytics infrastructure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Analytics information is retained according to the retention settings configured for our Google Analytics property and the applicable data-handling practices of Google.

Players can contact us to request information regarding their data or to request deletion of information associated with them, where applicable.

Some analytics information may be aggregated or may not be directly identifiable to an individual player.

We will review privacy requests and respond in accordance with applicable laws and the technical capabilities of our analytics services.

Clearing application data or uninstalling the game may remove locally stored information, but does not automatically guarantee deletion of information previously transmitted to Firebase Analytics.
